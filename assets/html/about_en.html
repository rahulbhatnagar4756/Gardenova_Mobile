About Gardenova

Your ultimate companion for smart plant care and professional gardening management.

Our Mission

Gardenova is designed to make plant care simple, intelligent, and accessible for everyone. Whether you are a beginner looking to keep your first houseplant alive or a professional landscaper offering premium gardening services, Gardenova provides the right tools to connect, monitor, and grow.

We combine artificial intelligence with professional horticultural knowledge to ensure your green spaces flourish.

Key Features

  • AI Plant Diagnosis: Instantly identify plant diseases and receive step-by-step treatment guides.
  • Smart Plant Reminders: Keep your plants hydrated and fertilized with custom schedules.
  • Professional Connections: Find and request quotes from vetted landscaping professionals in your area.
  • Soil Analysis: Get detailed local soil reports based on your geographic location.
